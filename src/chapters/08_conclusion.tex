\chapter*{Conclusion and Future Perspectives}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Conclusion and Future Perspectives}

This six-month final-year internship at Rakuten France provided me with a valuable opportunity to experience frontend development within a large-scale professional environment and to apply the knowledge acquired throughout my academic studies to real-world engineering challenges. Working within the Architecture team allowed me to gain a deeper understanding of how technical decisions, development practices, and infrastructure contribute to the reliability and evolution of a complex frontend platform.

Throughout the internship, I contributed to several projects covering different aspects of frontend engineering. The implementation of dark mode across multiple applications allowed me to explore the existing frontend ecosystem and work on shared components and performance optimization. The modernization of development tools, including Node.js, pnpm, and Storybook, helped me better understand the importance of maintaining a reliable and up-to-date development environment. I also worked on improving software quality through the implementation of visual regression testing with Reg-Viz, end-to-end testing with Playwright, and CI/CD automation using GitHub Actions. Finally, the development of a proxy for securing access to Google Cloud Storage allowed me to explore the full software lifecycle, from development and testing to deployment.

Beyond the technical skills acquired, this internship also strengthened my ability to work independently, investigate unfamiliar codebases, solve practical problems, and collaborate within a professional team. It allowed me to better understand the importance of code quality, maintainability, testing, documentation, and automation when working on software used at a large scale. The challenges encountered throughout the different tasks also taught me to approach problems progressively, evaluate possible solutions, and consider both immediate requirements and long-term maintainability.

Overall, this internship successfully fulfilled its objective of providing me with practical experience in a professional frontend development environment. It allowed me to consolidate the knowledge gained during my Master's degree while discovering new technologies, tools, and engineering practices. More importantly, it gave me a clearer understanding of the responsibilities and expectations associated with working as a software engineer and provided a strong foundation for my future career in software development.
